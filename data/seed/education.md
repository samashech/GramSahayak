# GramSahayak Education Guide for Students and Parents (education)

Scholarships and school schemes for rural students. Always check the current deadline and eligibility on the official portal before applying. NSP helpline: 0120-6619540, email helpdesk@nsp.gov.in.

## National Scholarship Portal (NSP)

### What it is
scholarships.gov.in is one online window for central ministry scholarships, many state scholarships and UGC/AICTE scholarships. Money is paid by DBT to the student's Aadhaar-linked bank account.

### How to apply on NSP
1. One Time Registration (OTR): register on scholarships.gov.in or the NSP OTR mobile app with Aadhaar (or Aadhaar enrolment ID) and the mobile number; complete face authentication if asked. You get an OTR number valid for your whole study period.
2. Log in with the OTR number and fill the application: personal, academic, bank and income details.
3. Select the scholarship you are eligible for (pre-matric, post-matric, merit-cum-means, top class, AICTE schemes).
4. Upload documents and submit before the deadline (usually October to November for fresh applications; check the portal).
5. The school or college verifies the application, then the district / state nodal officer verifies it. Track status on the portal.

### Documents usually needed
Aadhaar card, income certificate, caste certificate (if applicable), previous year marksheet, bonafide certificate or fee receipt from the institution, Aadhaar-linked bank account, passport-size photograph.

## PM YASASVI (OBC, EBC and DNT students)
PM Young Achievers Scholarship Award Scheme for Vibrant India supports students from Other Backward Classes (OBC), Economically Backward Classes (EBC) and De-notified, Nomadic and Semi-Nomadic Tribes (DNT) with annual family income up to Rs 2.5 lakh. Components: pre-matric scholarship for Classes 9 and 10, post-matric scholarship from Class 11 onwards, and Top Class School and Top Class College scholarships for students admitted to notified top institutions. Apply on the National Scholarship Portal.

## Scholarships for SC and ST students

### Pre-Matric (Classes 9 and 10)
For SC and ST students in Classes 9 and 10 whose parents' annual income is up to Rs 2.5 lakh. Provides an annual scholarship and allowances. Apply on NSP or the state scholarship portal.

### Post-Matric (Class 11 to PhD)
For SC and ST students studying after Class 10, with parents' annual income up to Rs 2.5 lakh. Covers compulsory non-refundable fees and an academic allowance. Many states run this on their own portals (for example MahaDBT in Maharashtra); check your state portal and NSP.

## AICTE Pragati Scholarship for Girls
For girl students admitted to the first year (or second year by lateral entry) of an AICTE-approved degree or diploma course in technical education. Rs 50,000 per year for each year of study. Annual family income must be up to Rs 8 lakh; up to two girls per family. About 5,000 scholarships every year. Apply on NSP under the AICTE Pragati scheme. Related: Saksham scholarship for specially-abled students, Swanath for orphans and wards of martyrs.

## PM-POSHAN (school meals)
Pradhan Mantri Poshan Shakti Nirman (earlier the Mid-Day Meal scheme) gives one free hot cooked meal on every school day to children in Bal Vatika (pre-primary) and Classes 1 to 8 in government and government-aided schools. No application is needed; the child only needs to be enrolled and attend school. Complaints can be made to the School Management Committee or the block education officer.

## Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST)
Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas are free residential schools (Class 6 to 12) with free education, boarding, lodging and uniforms, in almost every district.
- Class 6 admission: through JNVST for students studying in Class 5 in the same district. At least 75% of seats are reserved for children from rural areas; one-third of seats are for girls.
- The test checks mental ability, arithmetic and language; it is held in regional languages.
- Apply online on navodaya.gov.in when the admission notice is released (usually mid-year). There is no application fee.
- Lateral entry tests are also held for Class 9 and Class 11.
